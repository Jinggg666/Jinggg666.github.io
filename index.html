Programs I've Participated In

Giving back and growing through community

Youth Leadership Millennium (YLM)

S.U.C.C.E.S.S.  |  Funded by Canada Service Corps

YLM is a free, year-long leadership development program that brings together youth aged 12–30 from all cultural backgrounds. Through workshops, community service projects, environmental education, and team-building experiences, participants develop real-world skills in communication, collaboration, and civic responsibility. The program centres on four pillars: reconciliation, building an inclusive Canada, environmental preservation, and strengthening youth resilience.

By participating, I contribute to my community through 120+ hours of volunteer service, help promote inclusivity and cross-cultural understanding, and take active steps toward environmental stewardship — building the leadership mindset needed to create lasting, positive change.
